SaiA

System AI Assistant

Welcome — this is your own corner of the computer. A quiet assistant that sits nearby, listens when you need it, and gets out of the way when you don't. Built to manage your machine with ease, speed, and a little bit of style.

How it works

Three ways to reach it. One thing it does.

SaiA isn't a browser tab you forget to close. It lives on your desktop, in whichever shape fits the moment — then quietly does what you asked.

01

Full window

Open it up when you want the full picture — a proper console to type into and a running log of everything you've asked it to do.

02

Floating orb

Shrink it to a small circle that sits on top of whatever you're working on. Drag it anywhere. Click it whenever you need it back.

03

Compact bar

A single slim line for quick, one-off requests — no window to manage, just type and go.

1

Say what you need. Plain language, typed in — no commands to memorize.

2

SaiA figures it out. It reads the request and works out the right action.

3

It just happens. Apps open, tasks run, answers appear — right there in the log.
